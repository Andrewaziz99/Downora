Downora third-party notices
===========================

Downora is a front end. It runs the programs below, which are installed in its "tools" folder and copied to
%LocalAppData%\Downora\tools the first time it starts. Each keeps its own license. Downora does not change them.

yt-dlp

  https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp

FFmpeg and FFprobe

  (ffmpeg-master-latest-win64-lgpl), which contains no GPL or nonfree components.
  License: GNU Lesser General Public License, version 2.1 or later.
  FFmpeg is a trademark of Fabrice Bellard, originator of the FFmpeg project.
  You may replace these two files with your own build: Settings > Tools > "Use my own FFmpeg".
  The license text is at https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
  Source code: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html and https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
  Downora uses FFmpeg and FFprobe only as separate programs (it starts them and reads their output); it does not link to them.
